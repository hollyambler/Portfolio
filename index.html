About

I'm a senior UX researcher with nine years at Microsoft, most of it spent inside enterprise and security products where the users are experts, the workflows are complex, and a confusing interface has real operational cost.

My strength is reframing the question. Teams often come to research asking "does this design work?" — and the more valuable answer is usually "here's what your customers are actually trying to do, and whether this is even the right thing to build." I work across generative and evaluative methods: design reviews, in-depth interviews, concept testing, diary studies, and survey work, and I translate what I hear into direction product and design teams can move on.

I care a lot about rigor and honesty in research — separating what customers say from what they need, presenting evidence without overclaiming, and making sure a study changes a decision rather than just decorating one.

I'm currently open to senior and lead UX research roles. If you're building something complex for demanding users, that's exactly where I do my best work.
